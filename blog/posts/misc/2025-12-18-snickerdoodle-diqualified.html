Snickerdoodle Disqualified

December 18, 2025

Partner came into my office this evening while I was talking to Gemini about a web-based terminal application, don't ask, and queried me the following hypothetical:

Pretend you are a cookie judge and you know all the rules like pastries aren't cookies, etc. Why would a Snickerdoodle be disqualified?

Without any hesitation, I responded with: "It doesn't have chocolate chips", which wasn't the punchline she wanted, though she laughed anyway. Apparently this joke has something to do with cookies can't be candy dogs... snicker... doodle... I mostly get it though it's not particularly funny.

Anyway, since I was staring at an empty Gemini text input I decided to put the exact same question to it and wanted to share one of it's four criteria for disqualifying the Snickerdoodle:

The Infraction: If the Snickerdoodle uses a laminated dough technique (layering cold butter to create steam-filled pockets), it is no longer a cookie; it is a flattened croissant disguised with cinnamon sugar.

The Rule: Cookies should have a uniform, dense crumb. If I see distinct, flaky layers that require a fork to manage the debris, it’s a pastry. DQ’d.

I, what?

And having to use a fork to manage debris made me cry with laughter again.

It's moments like this where I appreciate the AI. I've had a whole blog post brewing in my head about when and how I use AI in my work and my personal projects, and my reasoning is pretty nuanced, but random uses like this are my favorite. I'm a huge fan of unexpected humor and when it can suddenly crack open the moment and insert something completely ridiculous I get one of those down-in-my-belly laughs and tears running down my face and find myself really enjoying the moment.

Snickerdoodles will henceforth be forever known as flattened croissants.
